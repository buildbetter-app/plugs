Fresh-store certificate bootstrap is a separate follow-up, outside this fix.
It could certify the fully validated proposed graph during the initial ordinary root publication.
It would require atomic typed graph publication, authoritative witnesses and source fences.
Imported stores would instead need a separately designed import-time semantic certificate.
Absent such a certificate, the first parented create retains one complete initialization census.
On a very large uncertified store, that first census may exceed the unchanged 2 s preparation budget.
